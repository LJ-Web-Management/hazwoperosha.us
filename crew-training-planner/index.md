## Your crew's order

Enter worker counts above to see the courses and total.

Every seat is billed at list price. Workers who supervise also need worker-level training; incident commanders need at least operations-level training before the incident command course. Check each role against your site safety and health plan. Purchase orders: [+1-866-429-6742](tel:+18664296742).

## How to use the results

- Count each worker once, in the role that matches their main duties.
- For workers who already have training, use the refresher rows. See [onboarding new hires](https://www.hazwoperosha.us/guides/onboarding-new-hires-with-prior-hazwoper-training/) for accepting prior training.
- Supervised field experience and site-specific training happen on your site after the online courses.
- Not sure which role fits? Read [training by industry](https://www.hazwoperosha.us/guides/hazwoper-training-by-industry/) or call us.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the planner place an order?

No. It adds the courses and seats to your cart. You review everything and pay at checkout.

### Are the prices in the planner final?

Yes. The planner uses the same list price per seat as checkout, with no volume discount, so the total matches your cart.

### What if a role is missing?

Choose the closest role, or call +1-866-429-6742 and we will help you match training to the job.
