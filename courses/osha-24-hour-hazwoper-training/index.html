OSHA 24 Hour HAZWOPER Training

OSHA 24 Hour HAZWOPER Training is a 24-hour online course that meets 29 CFR 1910.120(e), for individual workers or whole crews. Typical learners: environmental remediation, occasional-exposure site workers. Order one seat or a seat for every worker in a single checkout. Each learner trains at their own pace and gets a certificate and wallet card on passing.
