RCRA Universal Waste Handler Training

RCRA Universal Waste Handler Training is a 2-hour online course that meets 40 CFR 273 (RCRA), for individual workers or whole crews. Typical learners: retail, facilities management, battery/lamp/e-waste handlers. Order one seat or a seat for every worker in a single checkout. Each learner trains at their own pace and gets a certificate and wallet card on passing.
