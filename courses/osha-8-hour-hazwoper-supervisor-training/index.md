# OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Supervisor Training

29 CFR 1910.120(e)(4)

OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Supervisor Training is a 8-hour online course that meets 29 CFR 1910.120(e)(4), for individual workers or whole crews. Typical learners: on-scene incident commanders/supervisors, remediation site leads. Order one seat or a seat for every worker in a single checkout. Each learner trains at their own pace and gets a certificate and wallet card on passing.
