# OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

29 CFR 1910.120(q)(6)(i)

OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training is a 4-hour online course that meets 29 CFR 1910.120(q)(6)(i), for individual workers or whole crews. Typical learners: first responders, security, facility staff who may witness a release. Order one seat or a seat for every worker in a single checkout.
