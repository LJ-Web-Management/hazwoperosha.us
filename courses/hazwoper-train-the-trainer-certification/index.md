# HAZWOPER Train-the-Trainer Certification

29 CFR 1910.120(e)

HAZWOPER Train-the-Trainer Certification is a 24-hour online course that meets 29 CFR 1910.120(e), for individual workers or whole crews. Typical learners: in-house HAZWOPER trainers/EHS managers. Order one seat or a seat for every worker in a single checkout. Each learner trains at their own pace and gets a certificate and wallet card on passing.

## How ordering works

1. Add seats for everyone who needs this course, plus any other courses, to one cart.
2. Check out once by card, or call for purchase-order billing.
3. Send learner names after the order. Each worker trains at their own pace and downloads a certificate and wallet card on passing.
