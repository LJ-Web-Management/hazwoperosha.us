Employer FAQ

Can I buy training for my whole crew in one order?

Yes. Add every course your crew needs to the cart, set the number of seats for each course, and check out once. The crew training planner builds the cart for you from roles and headcounts.

Can we pay by purchase order or invoice?

Yes. Call +1-866-429-6742 or email sales@hazwoper-osha.com for purchase-order billing. Card payments are taken securely at checkout.

Do I need every learner's name at checkout?

No. Check out with the buyer's details and the number of seats. Learner names and emails can be sent after the order is confirmed.

Is there a volume discount?

Every seat is billed at the list price shown, whatever the crew size, so quotes and budgets are simple to work out.

Will OSHA accept online HAZWOPER training for my workers?

Online training can cover the classroom portion. You still provide supervised field experience, hands-on practice with your equipment and site-specific training.

Our new hires trained elsewhere. Do they have to retrain?

Not necessarily. Under 1910.120(e)(9) you can accept documented equivalent training, but new hires still need site-specific training, field experience at your site and a current annual refresher.

Do you have training for Spanish-speaking crews?

Yes. The OSHA 40-Hour HAZWOPER course is available in Spanish, and Spanish and English seats can go in the same order.

Which courses do California crews need?

California work falls under Cal/OSHA 8 CCR 5192. Cal/OSHA 40 hour, 24 hour and 8 hour refresher courses are available, along with TSDF versions.
