# Budgeting for HAZWOPER Training: A Worked Example for a 10-Person Crew

Employer guide · 2 min read · Updated 2026-10-30

HAZWOPER training costs come in two waves: a larger first-year spend for initial training and a smaller, predictable yearly spend for refreshers. Here's how to plan both, using our list prices. Every seat is billed at the list price, whatever the crew size.

## Costs beyond the course fee

- **Paid training time**: a 40-hour course is a full work week per person. Budget wages for it.
- **Field experience**: 3 days (40-hour) or 1 day (24-hour) on site under a trained supervisor, usually less productive time.
- **Medical surveillance**: baseline and annual exams for workers who need them.
- **Respiratory protection**: medical evaluations, annual fit tests and respirators.
- **Site-specific training**: a few hours at each new site.
- **Turnover**: new hires without certificates need initial training. Budget for your typical hiring rate.

## Ways to keep costs predictable

- **Put the whole crew on one refresher cycle** and order everyone's seats in one cart once a year.
- **Check prior training** before buying initial courses for experienced hires: see [onboarding new hires](https://www.hazwoperosha.us/guides/onboarding-new-hires-with-prior-hazwoper-training/).
- **Match the level to the job**: don't buy 40-hour seats for people who only need awareness training, and don't under-train people who need 40 hours.
- **Self-paced online training** lets workers train in slow periods instead of losing a block of days to a classroom course.
- **Consider train-the-trainer** if you hire often: [HAZWOPER Train-the-Trainer Certification](https://www.hazwoperosha.us/courses/hazwoper-train-the-trainer-certification/) ($399.99).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does HAZWOPER training cost per worker?

It depends on the level: the 40 hour course, 24 hour course and 8 hour refresher each have a fixed per-seat list price on this site. Budget wages for training time, field experience, medical surveillance and respirator fit testing on top.

### Is there a discount for training a whole crew?

Every seat is billed at the list price shown, whatever the crew size, so budgets and quotes are simple. Purchase-order billing is available by phone.

### What ongoing HAZWOPER costs should employers expect?

An 8 hour refresher for every 40-hour and 24-hour trained worker each year, supervisor refreshers, annual medical exams and fit tests where required, and initial training for new hires.
