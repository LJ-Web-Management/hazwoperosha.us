# HAZWOPER Training for Spanish-Speaking and Multilingual Crews

Employer guide · 2 min read · Updated 2026-10-30

Training only counts if workers understand it. OSHA has long said that employers must present safety training in a manner employees can understand, which means in a language and vocabulary they understand. For crews with Spanish-speaking or multilingual members, that shapes how you choose and deliver HAZWOPER training.

## What OSHA expects

In a 2010 enforcement memorandum, OSHA directed inspectors to check that training required by its standards is presented in a way employees can understand. If a worker can't read or understand the language of the course, the training doesn't meet the requirement, even if they sat through it.

## Options for Spanish-speaking crews

- **Spanish-language course**: [OSHA 40-Hour HAZWOPER Training (Spanish)](https://www.hazwoperosha.us/courses/osha-40-hour-hazwoper-training-spanish/) ($249.99) covers the full 40 hours in Spanish.
- **Bilingual supervisors** for field experience, site-specific training and briefings.
- **Translated site documents**: key parts of the site safety and health plan, emergency procedures and decon steps.

## Checking comprehension

- Ask workers to explain or demonstrate key procedures in their own words: donning and doffing PPE, decon steps, evacuation signals.
- Use the final exam in the worker's language, and review missed topics one to one.
- Watch the first field days closely and correct misunderstandings on the spot.

## Site communication for mixed-language crews

- Use pictogram-based signs and labels, and point to GHS pictograms during briefings.
- Agree on simple hand signals for exclusion zone work, where respirators make speech hard.
- Pair workers in the buddy system so each pair can communicate.
- Make sure emergency alarms and evacuation signals are understood by everyone, regardless of language.

## Literacy, not only language

Some workers speak a language fluently but read it with difficulty. Mix written material with demonstrations, visuals and hands-on practice, and give extra time where needed.

## Ordering for a mixed crew

Add Spanish and English 40-hour seats to the same cart, along with refreshers and supervisor courses, and check out once. The [crew training planner](https://www.hazwoperosha.us/crew-training-planner/) has a separate line for Spanish-speaking workers.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does OSHA require training in Spanish for Spanish-speaking workers?

OSHA requires training to be presented in a language and vocabulary workers understand. For workers who understand Spanish better than English, that means Spanish-language training or effective interpretation.

### Is the 40 hour HAZWOPER course available in Spanish?

Yes. The OSHA 40-Hour HAZWOPER Training is available in a full Spanish-language version.

### How can employers confirm workers understood the training?

Have workers explain or demonstrate key procedures, review missed exam topics, and watch closely during supervised field experience.
